## 🧭 How Every Boss Note Is Organized

Every subtopic file follows a strict, predictable layout so you can study at full speed or jump straight to what you need:

1. **Header Block:** Tells you the parent topic, data-driven exam priority (`🔴` / `🟠` / `🟡` / `🟢`), exact exam frequency across the 7 finals and 4 CTs, and exact textbook/slide/class-note source citations.
2. **Why This Topic Matters:** A 2–4 sentence briefing on how RUET examiners test the subtopic and where it sits in Section A or Section B.
3. **📝 Key Definitions:** Direct, word-for-word quotes from Silberschatz, Korth & Sudarshan's *Database System Concepts* (7th Ed.) with section and printed page numbers cited. Use these verbatim definitions to open your exam answers and secure full definition marks.
4. **Core Theory & Step-by-Step Mechanics:** Conversational, zero-fluff explanations of every rule, algorithm, SQL clause, relational algebra operator, normalization test, and concurrency protocol, paired with verified diagrams.
5. **Worked Examples & Exam Variants Table:** Step-by-step walkthroughs of the flagship exam problem plus a quick-reference table comparing every year's data variant and final result.
6. **🏆 Golden Questions (Past Exam Archive) / 🔮 Likely Questions:** Every single past semester-final and class-test question for that subtopic, quoted **character-for-character** from the question paper (preserving original tables and notes) and followed by a **complete, exam-ready explanation-style solution written inline**. For slide/blueprint subtopics that have never appeared in a final (`⭐`), we provide 2–3 high-probability **Likely Questions** with full inline answers.
7. **⚡ Exam Tips & Common Mistakes:** Specific traps to avoid in the exam hall (such as the MySQL `FULL OUTER JOIN` workaround, the 6e relational algebra update syntax, and the TSO vs. `wait-die`/`wound-wait` rollback timestamp trap).
8. **🔗 Related Topics & Navigation Chain:** Every file is linked in a continuous `Previous ← | 🏠 Index | → Next` chain from `T01.01` to `T13.04`.

## 🛠️ How to Use This Resource

1. **Start at the [Master Index (`00_Index.md`)](00_Index.md):** Pick the triage path that matches your remaining prep time (**7+ Days**, **3 Days**, or **1 Night**).
2. **Study Active Problem-Solving First:** Database Systems is a **problem-solving exam**. Over **75% of the marks** come from writing SQL queries (`T05`, `T06`, `T07`), writing Relational Algebra expressions (`T04`), drawing E-R diagrams (`T02`), normalizing tables/FD sets (`T08`), and tracing precedence graphs or concurrency schedules (`T10`, `T11`). Do not just read the solutions. Write the SQL, RA, and graphs on paper first, then check the Golden Question answer.
3. **Keep the [Master Formula & Syntax Sheet (`99_Master_Formula_and_Syntax_Sheet.md`)](99_Master_Formula_and_Syntax_Sheet.md) Open:** Use it during revision to drill all RA symbols, MySQL 8 DDL/DML/Trigger/Procedure templates, Armstrong's axioms, closure & normal-form tests, conflict rules, TSO timestamp rules, and RAID formulas in one place.
